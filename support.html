Help & support

Back to the music.

Need a hand? Email andresboedo@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. Only attach documents or screenshots you are comfortable sharing.

Import a sheet

Tap Import and choose a PDF or image from Files. Stage Turner reads your documents; it does not include a sheet music catalog.

Use face gestures

Enable Gestures, grant camera access, and choose Head turn, Wink, or Mouth sideways. Face the camera in good lighting and pause in a neutral position for calibration. Adjust sensitivity and cooldown in Tuning if needed.

You can read and use manual navigation without camera access. Change camera permission in iPhone Settings.

Move through an enlarged sheet

Pinch to zoom, then use a gesture or Previous/Next to move through the content. A boundary message means you have reached the available edge. Drag to position an enlarged image manually.

Understand sheet unlocks

The free allowance covers three distinct imported files. A multi-page PDF counts as one file, not one unlock per page. Reopening identical file contents does not spend another allowance. A 50 Song Pack adds 50 file unlocks; Lifetime Access removes the limit. There is no subscription.

Restore a purchase

Open the purchase screen and choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account used for the purchase. If access is still missing, contact support. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Manage files and privacy

Remove sheets from the library to delete the app's copies; your original files are separate. See our privacy policy for camera, purchase, support and website data.
