Privacy policy

Your music. Your device.

Effective September 11, 2026. This policy covers Stage Turner and this support website. Contact the app developer at andresboedo@gmail.com about privacy or data requests.

Files and reading preferences

Stage Turner stores imported PDFs and images, library information, preferences and local sheet-unlock records on your device. The app does not upload your sheet files to a Stage Turner server. Your operating system's backup features may include app data, depending on your settings.

Camera and face gestures

Camera access is optional and enables hands-free navigation. Camera frames and facial movement measurements are processed on your device to detect gestures. Stage Turner does not record camera video, upload camera frames or face measurements, or use them to identify you. You can disable gestures or revoke camera permission in Settings.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes in-app payments. Stage Turner uses RevenueCat to verify purchases and provide purchased access. This involves purchase and transaction information, an app-specific customer identifier, device/app information, and purchase or paywall interaction information used by the SDK. Network requests also expose network information such as an IP address to the receiving service. Stage Turner does not receive your payment-card details.

These services use data to process transactions, validate access and operate their services. Read Apple's privacy policy and RevenueCat's privacy policy for more information.

Advertising

Stage Turner does not include advertising or use camera data for advertising. The app does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Support

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate your request. You may request deletion of support correspondence; some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not include advertising, analytics scripts or our own tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information to host and secure the website. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

You can delete imported sheets in the app. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior. Purchase records handled by Apple and RevenueCat may remain available for purchase validation, restoration and applicable retention obligations. Contact us for help with a privacy request; deleting local app data does not erase a purchase from your Apple Account.

Updates

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
